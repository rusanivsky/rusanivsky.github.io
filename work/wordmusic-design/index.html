Design

word&music

Identity and concert graphics for word&music: a mark, a typeface drawn for the series and a system that turns every concert into posters, posts, stories and banners.

Client
word&music
Role
Visual identity and posters

Mark

The wordmark sets word&music in one line, with the ampersand binding the two words. Two short forms work where space is small: w&m, and the monogram wm, which is also the favicon.

Tembrava Display

The series has its own typeface: a high-contrast Regular with full Ukrainian Cyrillic and an extended Latin. Concert titles, names and dates are set in it.

In the internal Full build, typed word&music becomes the mark through discretionary ligatures; w&m works the same way. The Retail build licensed to others contains no marks.

Tembrava Display licence

System

The grid, type sizes and placement stay fixed from concert to concert. Each concert gets its own ground, ink and one form, and every layout exists in a light and a night version.
