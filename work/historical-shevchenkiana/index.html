Historical Shevchenkiana

Cover design for the bibliographic index “Historical Shevchenkiana” for the Historical Library, 2016. A classical treatment in soft colours, as befits an academic reference edition. The front carries a portrait of Taras Shevchenko and a sample of his handwriting; the back, his painting of the Exaltation Monastery in Poltava.
