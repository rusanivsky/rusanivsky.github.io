History of Ukraine

“History of Ukraine” is a series of annual bibliographic indexes published by the National Historical Library of Ukraine — one volume a year, each gathering the year’s writing on the country’s history. The cover for the 2020 volume is built around a 1918 map of Ukraine found in the Ivan Franko Ukrainian Centre library in Richmond, Canada.
