About me

Kyrylo Rusanivsky

I edit videos. I design and typeset books. I photograph people and events. I work on interviews, YouTube series and social-media videos; I create publications and printed materials. I also keep a street-photography series about the city, spontaneous scenes and landscapes. I work remotely.

I have worked remotely, as a freelancer, since 2013. Right now I take on video editing and design projects — from a single task to ongoing support. I work as a registered sole proprietor in Ukraine: a contract or a public offer, an invoice, payment to a business account.
