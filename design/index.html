Історія України. 2020

Cover design for the bibliographic index “Istoriia Ukrainy. 2020” for the National Historical Library of Ukraine. Built around a 1918 map of Ukraine found in the Ivan Franko Ukrainian Centre library in Richmond, Canada.

Історична Шевченкіана

Cover design for the bibliographic index “Istorychna Shevchenkiana” for the Historical Library, 2016. A classical treatment in soft colours, as befits an academic reference edition. The front carries a portrait of Taras Shevchenko and a sample of his handwriting; the back, his painting of the Exaltation Monastery in Poltava.
